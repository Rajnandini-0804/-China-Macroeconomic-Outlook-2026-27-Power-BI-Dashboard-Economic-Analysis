
📊 **China Macroeconomic Outlook 2026–27 | Power BI Dashboard & Economic Analysis**

I’m excited to share my latest **Data Analytics & Power BI project**, focused on analyzing China’s economy using macroeconomic data from **2015–2025**.

As part of this project, I created:

🔹 An interactive **Power BI Dashboard (.pbix)**  
🔹 A detailed **China Macroeconomic Outlook 2026–27 Word Report**  
🔹 KPI cards with year-on-year comparisons  
🔹 Historical trend analysis  
🔹 Economic and business-impact analysis  

The dashboard provides an executive-level view across seven key areas:

• Economic Growth & Demand  
• Inflation & Prices  
• Fiscal Health  
• Structural Risk  
• External Sector  
• Monetary & Financial Conditions  
• Labor Market  

I analyzed key indicators including **Real GDP Growth, Core CPI Inflation, Policy Rate, Government Debt/GDP, Unemployment Rate, Consumption Growth, Labor Productivity, Private Investment, Fiscal Balance, Current Account, External Debt, Credit Growth, Real Wage Growth, Labor Force Participation, Economic Policy Uncertainty, and Geopolitical Risk Exposure**.

The project also explores China-specific structural themes such as **property-sector pressures, government and local debt risks, external trade, RMB conditions, R&D, technology, productivity, demographics, and long-term growth challenges**.

For the analysis, I referred to reliable sources including the **National Bureau of Statistics of China (NBS), People’s Bank of China (PBOC), Ministry of Finance, SAFE, IMF, and World Bank**, depending on the indicator.

The main objective was not only to visualize the data, but also to understand **what changed, why it changed, how the changes affect China’s economy, and what they could mean for businesses and the 2026–27 outlook**.

**Power BI | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Dashboard Design | Macroeconomic Analysis | Business Insights**
